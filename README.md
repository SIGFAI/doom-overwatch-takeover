# Overwatch Takeover

**Doom** × Overwatch · visual · idea by the team

Bring Overwatch into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I dropped Overwatch heroes into Doom: Roadhog hooks you, Mercy keeps resurrecting the dead, and my pulse bomb ended up as Play of the Game.

## Install

Put mod.pk3 next to GZDoom and run it with Freedoom 2: gzdoom -iwad freedoom2.wad -file mod.pk3. Heroes replace Doom's monsters; shoot with the Pulse Pistols, fill the ultimate meter with kills, and press U to throw the pulse bomb.
